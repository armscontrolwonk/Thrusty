RTV-560

Property: Specific Heat

<< Return to Material Data

Name: RTV-560
Database: NASA Ames Thermal Protection Materials
Category: Adhesives
Composition: Exact formulation is proprietary, but it is composed of ethyl silicate, diatomaceous earth, silanol/st dimethyldiphenylsiloxane, and red iron oxide. Dibutyl tin dilaurate (DBT) is used as a curing agent.
Manufacturer: General Electric
Technical Readiness Level: 9
Last Modified: 2001-10-01

Description:

  • RTV-560 is a room temperature cure silicone rubber that can be used in a variety of applications including the bonding of various thermal protection system (TPS) materials such as the ceramic tiles and blankets used on shuttle orbiters. ManufacturerÕs data indicates that it can withstand continuous duty at temperatures up to 500¡F and for short periods up to 600¡F.
