DURAFRSI Blanket

Property: Density

<< Return to Material Data

Name: DURAFRSI Blanket
Database: NASA Ames Thermal Protection Materials
Category: Silicon-Based Reusable Composites: Composite Flexible Blankets
Composition: Hybrid Nextel 440 / Inconel 600 / B-Au5 outer fabric, Nextel 440 inner fabric, and Saffil alumina insulation, sewn together with a Nextel 440 thread.
Manufacturer: Experimental material-not currently manufacture
Technical Readiness Level: 7
Last Modified: 2001-10-01

Description:

  • A development of the basic AFRSI TPS architecture, DurAFRSI is a quilted blanket TPS consisting of a woven hybrid alumina-borosilicate/Inconel wire/braze alloy wire outer fabric, a woven alumina-borosilicate inner fabric, and fibrous alumina batting sewn together with alumina-borosilicate thread. An Inconel foil (0.004Ó typical) is brazed on the surface of the blanket after the sewing operation. No water-proofing agent is applied to DurAFRSI at the factory or between flights; the superalloy outer surface is waterproof. Compared to tile-type TPS, DurAFRSI's installed cost is relatively low, but its aerodynamic capabilities are somewhat more limited. It has a more highly catalytic surface and a lower emissivity, both of which can lead to amplified surface heating. Additionally, DurAFRSI has lower tensile and shear strength compared to tiles, making it less applicable in areas of high aerodynamic loading; especially rapidly fluctuating aerodynamic loads.
