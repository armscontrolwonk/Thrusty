TABI Blanket

Property: Emissivity

<< Return to Material Data

Name: TABI Blanket
Database: NASA Ames Thermal Protection Materials
Category: Silicon-Based Reusable Composites: Composite Flexible Blankets
Composition: Nextel 440 (or Nicalon NLM 202) woven core, Saffil alumina insulation
Manufacturer: No current manufacturer
Technical Readiness Level: 9
Last Modified: 2001-10-01

Description:

  • TABI is a blanket TPS consisting of a fluted core framework that is integrally woven from ceramic fibers (alumino-borosilicate and silicon carbide have each been used). This core framework has triangular cross-section channels that span the width of the blanket. These channels are formed by sections of fabric that connect the outer and inner fabrics; all of these are woven simultaneously as an integral unit. After weaving, the fluted core is stuffed with sections of fibrous alumina batting that has been shaped to completely fill the triangular channels. For most applications, a water-proofing agent would typically be applied to TABI at the factory and again between flights. A surface coating of a ceramic material such as C-9 may be used, but the need for such a coating is not as pronounced for TABI as for quilted-type blankets since TABI has superior durability to vibro-acoustic environments (as high as 170 db). In addition to meeting emittance requirements, the coating makes the outer surface more rigid and helps to protect the blanket's top surface fibers from aerodynamically-induced abrasion. Compared to tile-type TPS, TABI's installed cost is lower, but its aerodynamic capabilities are somewhat more limited. It has a slightly rougher surface finish than most tiles and therefore has an increased propensity to force a laminar boundary layer into turbulence. This increased roughness can also produce slightly amplified local heating where boundary layers are relatively thin, such as the windward side of most reentry vehicles. Additionally, TABI has lower tensile and shear strength compared to tiles, making it less applicable in areas of high aerodynamic loading; especially rapidly fluctuating aerodynamic loads. Property values given here are for a blanket constructed from a Nicalon NLM 202 SiC fabric.
